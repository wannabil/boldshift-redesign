FAQ

Common questions

Who is Bold Shift Advisory for?

Graduates, young professionals and aspiring managers preparing for interviews, and organizations that want practical workplace training for their people. It is founded and run by Ferhan Johar.

What does Ferhan's experience cover?

More than 15 years across MNC banking and regional corporate operations, including leading teams, hiring and developing people. The teaching draws on that hiring-side perspective.

How much is the Interview Ready Guide?

RM20. It is a 61-page PDF, emailed to you after payment.

Is the corporate training HRDC claimable?

Not confirmed yet. Ferhan's HRDC accreditation process is in progress, and program availability and any claimable status will be confirmed before booking.

Are the free tools really free?

Yes. The interview preparation checklist and the Interview Companion are free with no account needed, and your preparation stays in your browser on your device.
